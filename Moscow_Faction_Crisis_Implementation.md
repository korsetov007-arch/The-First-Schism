# Moscow faction-control integration

Initial ownership: MOS 82 states, VOR 14 western/north-western states, OGO 12 northern/north-eastern states. DWA forms after the peaceful concession chain.

Crisis sequence: OGO chooses war or compromise on day 1. If it accepts compromise, states 10, 11 and 12 transfer to VOR at 75-day intervals. OGO can still declare war at each step. After all three concessions, Ilyich's Dark Watch seizes states 19–21 and declares war on VOR. MOS can unlock one mediation delay.

Static checks: 108 states, 906 province IDs, all IDs 1–906 covered once; owner counts MOS 82/VOR 14/OGO 12. The Clausewitz game parser has not yet been tested by launching HOI4.
